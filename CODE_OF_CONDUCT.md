# Code of Conduct

This project adapts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), licensed CC BY 4.0. The adapted text below is the operative version for EIS spaces.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in EIS a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the users of EIS.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Publishing another person's source code, credentials, logs or private project data without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious skills, backdoors, prompt-injection payloads or data-exfiltration code.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events and any space where an individual represents the project. It also applies when an individual's conduct could reasonably affect the safety of project participants.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this code, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers using the contact method listed in the repository profile or `SUPPORT.md`. Do not open a public issue.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter and the subject of the report.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

1. **Correction.** A private written warning for minor, first-time issues, explaining the impact and the expected behaviour. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a defined period of no interaction with those involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from interaction or contribution for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from participation for serious or repeated violations.

Security-related malicious contributions (backdoors, exfiltration, deliberate policy bypass) are treated as a permanent-ban case and may be reported to relevant hosting providers.
